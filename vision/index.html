Our focus

A classification suggestion needs source evidence and a reviewer. A site signal needs a route to staff response. A machine needs perception and control to turn observations into motion. The engineering lies in how these parts work together.

We start with the uncertainty, build a focused prototype and evaluate the combined system under defined conditions. Domain knowledge shapes what to test and what a useful result would mean.
