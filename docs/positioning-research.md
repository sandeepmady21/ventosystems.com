# Vento company positioning and design research

Reviewed 21 October 2026. This branch builds on `site-dept` at `01ace8957dfa2f5b2b80e89a8cc332d521a9e192`.

## Positioning

Vento is an interdisciplinary engineering company developing intelligent products and systems across business and the physical world. Its technical scope connects AI and data, software and integration, sensing and control, and systems engineering. These capabilities explain how the business areas relate; they are not a claim to a single shared software platform or expertise in every industry.

The public structure is four areas of work (Business, Private AI, Safety and Autonomy), a Lab for research and early work, and Vento Cyber as a sister company. Named products, client systems and physical prototypes make the breadth concrete. Their current development stages remain visible.

## Reference companies and lessons

These are lessons from official company materials, not endorsements or evidence of equivalent scale, facilities or credentials at Vento.

| Reference | Observed positioning | Lesson applied to Vento |
|---|---|---|
| [Atoms](https://atoms.co/) and its [vision](https://atoms.co/vision) | A shared mission connects named companies across domains. | Establish the umbrella first, then give each business a clear purpose and route to its work. |
| [Cambridge Consultants](https://www.cambridgeconsultants.com/about-us/) | Combines science and engineering with an explicit development model. Its [Ocado case](https://www.cambridgeconsultants.com/project/wireless-breakthrough-for-the-ocado-smart-platform/) identifies the specific wireless system it engineered and the constraints it addressed. | Explain the engineering contribution behind each project. A broad promise needs precise examples. |
| [TTP](https://www.ttp.com/our-approach-capabilities) | Builds multidisciplinary teams around a problem. Its [sensor-fusion case](https://www.ttp.com/case-studies/bespoke-sensor-fusion-enhanced-obstacle-detection-system) combines cameras, radar, compute and control, distinguishing tested applications from possible future uses. | Show which disciplines come together, how work is evaluated, and the difference between current evidence and an application being explored. |
| [SRI](https://www.sri.com/commercialization/) | Separates research, commercial R&D, ventures and licensing. [Synfini](https://www.sri.com/platform/synfini/) combines AI planning with automated chemistry. | Give products, custom systems and Lab work distinct roles within one company. |
| [Applied Intuition](https://www.appliedintuition.com/products) | Separates shared technical products from their industry applications. Its [Komatsu collaboration](https://www.appliedintuition.com/blog/applied-intuition-komatsu-partnership) connects software capabilities with a partner's mining knowledge. | Explain the transferable engineering expertise before listing applications. Avoid implying domain expertise everywhere. |
| [Intrinsic capabilities](https://www.intrinsic.ai/capabilities) and [Flowstate](https://www.intrinsic.ai/flowstate) | Makes perception, planning and sensor-based control tangible through tools and physical applications. | Connect technical disciplines to actual prototypes and workflows; use conceptual graphics as orientation, not as deployment evidence. |
| [Anduril's mission](https://www.anduril.com/mission) and [Lattice architecture](https://developer.anduril.com/guides/concepts/overview) | Explains a product development model and supports a shared technical foundation with named products and documentation. | Clarify Vento's own products, client work and research. Do not invent a universal platform to imitate this structure. |

## What changed in the site

- Company hero: “Intelligence, put to work.” The supporting sentence names AI, software and systems engineering.
- Shared engineering capabilities precede the four business areas.
- Selected work names the application, engineering disciplines and current stage: Travio, Practice Tracker, distribution operations, perception and control prototypes.
- Vision explains why interdisciplinary work matters, plus Vento's products, custom systems and Lab model.
- Business pages explain the problem, the combination of disciplines and what currently exists.
- Original technical illustrations provide a coherent visual language without pretending to be customer photographs or product screenshots.
- Lab and Cyber remain visible with their distinct relationships.
