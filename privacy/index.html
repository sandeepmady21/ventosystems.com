Privacy

What this website collects, and what happens when you write to us.

Who we are

This website is run by Vento Systems in the United States and Vento Systems Pvt Ltd in Hyderabad, India. Questions about privacy go to hello@ventosystems.com.

What this site collects

Nothing directly. The site has no sign-in, no analytics and no advertising trackers, and it sets no cookies.

Two services see standard request details, such as your IP address and browser, when you load a page: GitHub Pages, which hosts the site, and Google Fonts, which serves its typefaces.

The inquiry form

The form does not send anything to us. It opens a draft in your own email app, and nothing reaches us until you press send there.

When you email us

We use what you send to reply and to discuss the work you asked about. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.

We keep correspondence while it is useful for that conversation and our business records. To ask what we hold about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, write to hello@ventosystems.com.

Client systems

Systems we build or run for clients follow the data terms agreed with each client. This notice covers this website only.
