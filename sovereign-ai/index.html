Smaller, and better at one thing

The strongest AI for a narrow task is often not the biggest. Research teams keep finding the same pattern.

Weather

~10%

Europe’s weather centre, ECMWF, has run its own AI forecast model since February 2025. It reports higher skill than its physics-based model on most measures, with rain forecasts about 10% better: half a day to a day more warning.

ECMWF, 2025

Forecasting

1M

IBM’s Tiny Time Mixers have about a million parameters. On data they had never seen, they beat pre-trained forecasting models of up to 311 million parameters, and they run even on a machine without a GPU.

IBM Research, NeurIPS 2024

Software tools

7B

Gorilla, a 7-billion-parameter model fine-tuned to write API calls, beat GPT-4 at that one job and made up far fewer calls that do not exist.

UC Berkeley and Microsoft Research, 2023

Business tasks

+10 pts

Across 31 tasks, small 7-billion-parameter models, each fine-tuned for a single task, beat GPT-4 by 10 points on average.

Predibase, LoRA Land report, 2024

A narrow task, good data and a way to check each answer: that is how a small model beats a giant one. It is also faster and cheaper to run, and small enough to run on your own hardware. Results as reported by each research team.

What we build

A model made for your field, run where you choose, and owned by you.

01

Built for one field

A foundation model specialized for your industry’s work, learning your tasks from a handful of examples. On those tasks it can match a general model with far less compute, and it returns a decision with its confidence, not a paragraph.

02

Inside your walls

Models, search and the tools to run them, installed on your servers, in your data center or in an air-gapped network. Access controls, logs and the documents your security team needs to approve it.

03

Owned and improving

Your workflows become tests, your experts’ corrections become training data and production misses become improvements. The model and everything it learns are yours.

How it works

A loop that turns your experts’ knowledge into a model you own, and keeps it improving.

  1. 01Capture the workReal examples and the answers your experts expect become tests the model must pass.
  2. 02Train for your fieldA model adapted to your domain, measured against those tests and against a general model.
  3. 03Run inside your wallsDeployed on your hardware, online or fully offline.
  4. 04Learn from useCorrections and misses in daily work become new training data.
  5. 05Carry it forwardWhen a better base model arrives, your tests and data move with you.
