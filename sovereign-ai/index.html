Why vertical models win

Across very different fields, a model trained for one domain beats far larger general models at that domain’s work.

Weather

~10%

A model built only for weather now beats the physics-based system it learned from on most measures, with rain forecasts about 10% better.

Source: ECMWF, 2025

Forecasting

1M

A forecaster with about a million parameters beats pre-trained models up to 300 times its size on data it has never seen, and runs without a GPU.

Source: IBM Research, 2024

Software tools

7B

A model trained for one job, writing API calls, beat GPT-4 at that job and made up far fewer calls that do not exist.

Source: UC Berkeley, 2023

Business tasks

+10 pts

Across 31 tasks, small models each trained for a single task beat GPT-4 by 10 points on average.

Source: LoRA Land report, 2024

What we build

A vertical foundation model, deployed where your data lives, and owned by you.

01

A vertical foundation model

A foundation model specialized for your industry’s work, learning your tasks from a handful of examples. On those tasks it can match a general model with far less compute, and it returns a decision with its confidence, not a paragraph.

For exampleA claims model that approves, reviews or rejects; a tariff classifier for parts; a failure forecaster for pumps and turbines.

02

Air-gapped by design

Models, search and the tools to run them, installed on your servers, in your data center or in an air-gapped network. Access controls, logs and the documents your security team needs to approve it.

For exampleA plant network with no internet, a hospital data center, a defense supplier’s secure enclave.

03

Owned and improving

Your workflows become tests, your experts’ corrections become training data and production misses become improvements. The model and everything it learns are yours.

For exampleWhen a newer base model arrives, your tests and training data move to it, so nothing starts from scratch.

How it works

A loop that turns your experts’ knowledge into a model you own, and keeps it improving.

  1. 01Capture the workReal examples and the answers your experts expect become tests the model must pass.
  2. 02Train for your fieldA model adapted to your domain, measured against those tests and against a general model.
  3. 03Run where your data livesDeployed on your hardware, online or fully offline.
  4. 04Learn from useCorrections and misses in daily work become new training data.
  5. 05Carry it forwardWhen a better base model arrives, your tests and data move with you.

↻ Then again, with every correction your team makes.
