Engineering note / 01

From material data to a reviewable decision.

Travio is a client project for a US automotive-parts importer. It brings SAP material data, classification suggestions, duty estimates and supporting references into a workflow for human review.

The engineering focus

Keep the source record, suggested result and basis for review connected.

Vento Business →

A classification has to make sense to its reviewer.

A material record is the starting point. The useful output is a decision a person can inspect: which classification is suggested, what duty is estimated and which information supports the proposal.

The engineering problem crosses several boundaries. Business data supplies the context. The trade workflow defines the decision to be made. The interface has to bring both into the reviewer’s view.

What Vento built

The project connects SAP material data, classification suggestions, duty estimates and supporting references with human review. The reviewer’s decision becomes part of the traceable workflow.

That connection is the focus of this note. It shows how a business system and a domain-specific decision process come together in a single application.

The questions that shape evaluation

A useful review needs to account for incomplete records, competing interpretations and changes made by the person deciding. The evaluation questions below focus on the quality of that review.

Input quality

Does the material record contain enough context for a useful suggestion? How should an incomplete or ambiguous record be brought to the reviewer’s attention?

Traceability

Can the reviewer follow a suggestion back to the material record and the supporting references used to assess it?

Human judgment

Can the reviewer inspect the support, change the proposed decision and leave a clear account of the result?
