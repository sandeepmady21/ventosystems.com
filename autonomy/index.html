Vento Autonomy

From perception to purposeful motion.

We bring perception, embedded software and control together to investigate how machines act in the physical world. Our current work uses mobile robotics and controller firmware to connect these disciplines at the prototype scale.

Research & lab prototypes

Current work

Two starting points. A connected engineering problem.

A machine must interpret inputs, decide what to do and produce a controlled response. These lab projects give us concrete settings in which to investigate that chain.

01

Camera-guided mobile robot

The problem is to connect what a camera sees with a useful movement. Our mobile robot brings camera input, navigation and control into one small-scale system.

The prototype is a setting for studying the complete loop: perception, a movement decision and the machine’s response.

02

Controller firmware

A sit-to-stand walker brings a different control problem: translating inputs into predictable machine behaviour while keeping the person using it central to the design.

Our work is controller firmware. It provides a setting for studying how embedded software, sensing and control interact, and how the system should communicate its state.

Research directions

Applications for further research.

Haulage, warehouse movement and repetitive site tasks raise related questions in perception and control. These are research directions, with requirements and validation to be established for each setting.

01

Haulage and fixed routes

Investigate perception and collision-warning capabilities for vehicles in controlled operating areas, with any further automation developed through staged evaluation.

02

Warehouse movement

Explore the sensing and control problems involved in repetitive material movement, including how a system responds around people and changing obstacles.

03

Repetitive site tasks

Study opportunities in tasks such as marking out and moving material, where a constrained first application could make the work measurable.

Research methods

Study the whole loop.

Our research approach connects technical measurements with the task and the person operating the system. Supervised trials would follow a defined test scope and review.

01

Perception

Compare sensor observations with what is actually happening. Study missed detections, uncertainty and changing conditions before connecting an observation to an action.

02

Embedded control

Trace how inputs become commands and how the machine responds. Define operating limits and test the behaviour when information is delayed, incomplete or absent.

03

The operator interface

Make the machine’s state and intended action understandable. Define supervision and stop conditions alongside the control logic, then review the complete interaction.
