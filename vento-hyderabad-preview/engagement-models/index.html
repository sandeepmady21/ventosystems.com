Staff one workstream

Dedicated engineering teams

Give one product, platform or business workflow a cross-functional squad. Start with engineering, quality and technical leadership, then add design or data roles where the work calls for them.

Discuss a dedicated team ↗
Good fit
You need a team responsible for an ongoing workstream with its own goals.
How it operates
Your product owner sets priorities and acceptance criteria. Agree who leads day-to-day engineering and how the squad works with other teams.
First milestone
One end-to-end release with an agreed review and acceptance process.

Build a lasting engineering function

Offshore Development Center

Establish a Hyderabad engineering operation for sustained product development or R&D. Define its first remit and how shared engineering, platform and quality practices would support additional teams.

Discuss an offshore center ↗
Good fit
You have an engineering roadmap that calls for local leadership and operating support.
How it operates
Set reporting lines and local leadership. Assign responsibility for hiring, employment, workplace and IT to the parties supporting the setup.
First milestone
One engineering team delivering against its remit, with access, tooling and review in place.

Establish an India operation

Global Capability Center

Plan a Hyderabad center with a mandate from headquarters. Engineering can be the first function, with data, platform or other responsibilities added as the business needs them.

Discuss GCC planning ↗
Good fit
You want a long-term India operation with its own leadership and a mandate that can expand across functions.
How it operates
Define what the center can decide locally and how it reports. Agree which responsibilities your company, Vento and specialist partners would take on.
First milestone
A launch plan covering the first function, leader, reporting line, entity and setup responsibilities.

Plan to take ownership

Build–Operate–Transfer

Plan a staged setup for a team or center you intend to operate. Agree who builds and runs it first, who will receive it, and what readiness means before any handover.

Discuss a transfer plan ↗
Good fit
You want setup and operating support with a path to taking over the operation.
How it operates
Define which roles, contracts, equipment, systems and documentation could transfer. The parties agree the structure, terms and conditions for handover.
First milestone
An agreed setup plan and a checklist the receiving organization can use to assess readiness.

What to decide before setup

First deliverable

Give the team a concrete piece of work and name the person who can accept it.

Operating responsibilities

Agree who directs delivery, approves candidates, employs the team and manages local support.

Ownership and access

Set terms for IP, source code and data access. For a transfer, identify who will receive the operation and what they need to run it.

Common questions about the models

Can we start with a team and add a center later?

Yes, if the model and terms support it. Discuss future leadership, employment and ownership early so you know what would need to change when you expand.

How does an offshore development center differ from a GCC?

An offshore development center focuses on sustained engineering work. A GCC has a longer-term organizational mandate and can cover functions beyond engineering. Each setup needs clear reporting, operating responsibilities and ownership terms.

When do we decide who owns the code and data?

Agree IP terms, repository control and access rules during scoping. If there is a planned transfer, agree separately which operating assets and responsibilities would move at handover.

What should your Hyderabad team take on first?

Share the first deliverable and how you want the team to work. We can use that to discuss which setup fits.
