Five ways to engage

Match the team to the work.

Add specialist skills

Team extension

Add a specialist to a team with an established backlog and technical lead. A data engineer might own an integration, or a QA engineer might improve test coverage.

Your leads set priorities and review releases. Agree how candidates are assessed and who handles employment and ongoing support.

Staff one workstream

Dedicated engineering teams

Give one product, platform or business workflow a cross-functional squad. Start with engineering, quality and technical leadership, then add design or data roles where the work calls for them.

Your product owner sets priorities and acceptance criteria. Agree who leads day-to-day engineering and how the squad works with other teams.

Build a lasting engineering function

Offshore Development Center

Establish a Hyderabad engineering operation for sustained product development or R&D. Define its first remit and how shared engineering, platform and quality practices would support additional teams.

Set the reporting lines and decide who leads locally. Assign responsibility for hiring, employment, workplace and IT to the parties supporting the setup.

Establish an India operation

Global Capability Center

Plan a Hyderabad centre with a mandate from headquarters. Engineering can be the first function, with data, platform or other responsibilities added as the business needs them.

Define what the centre can decide locally, how it reports, and who leads its first functions. Agree which operating responsibilities your company and local partners would take on.

Plan to take ownership

Build–Operate–Transfer

Plan a staged setup for a team or centre you intend to operate. Agree who builds and runs it first, who will receive it, and what readiness means before any handover.

Define which roles, contracts, equipment, systems and documentation could transfer. The structure and terms depend on the parties involved and the scope they agree.

What to decide before setup.

First deliverable

Give the team a concrete piece of work and name the person who can accept it.

Operating responsibilities

Agree who directs delivery, approves candidates, employs the team and manages local support.

Ownership and access

Set terms for IP, source code and data access. For a transfer, identify who will receive the operation and what they need to run it.
