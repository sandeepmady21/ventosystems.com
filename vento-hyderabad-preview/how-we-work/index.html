From first brief to ongoing delivery

How the team comes together.

  1. Define the first scope

    Review the product or workflow, the systems it touches and the work that is waiting. Choose a first deliverable, the roles needed and the person who can review the result.

  2. Assess engineers against the work

    Build role profiles from real tasks. Agree how candidates are sourced, how technical skills are assessed, when client interviews happen and who approves each hire.

  3. Join your engineering workflow

    Set up approved access to the issue tracker, repositories and environments. Agree where code and data can be handled and who approves code reviews, releases and production access.

  4. Review what the team delivers

    Agree a reporting cadence and who resolves blockers. Review what shipped, defects or incidents, the next priorities, and any decisions the team needs from your leads.

  5. Expand where the mandate grows

    Use delivery reviews to decide when to add roles, take on a second workstream or introduce more local leadership. Confirm the team has the capacity and documentation to support the change.

  6. Transfer when agreed

    If you intend to take over the operation, identify who will receive it and how you will assess readiness. Plan which people, contracts, access and records could move, and how support will change.

Operating responsibilities

What we agree before setup.

Engineering decisions

Identify who owns the roadmap, approves hires and accepts releases. Agree the scope of technical leadership and how decisions or issues are escalated.

Code, data and IP

Record repository and environment ownership, how access is granted and removed, and the terms for work product and existing IP. Define what the team can do with production data.

Local operations

Assign recruiting, employment, payroll, equipment, workplace and compliance responsibilities among your company, Vento and any agreed providers. Agree costs alongside the scope.
