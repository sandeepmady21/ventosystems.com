Solutions

Three starting points for agency teams.

Document Assistants

Plain-language answers quoted from your manuals, standards and specifications, with the source page alongside. Questions the documents cannot answer go to a person.

Workflow Automation

Documents received, checked against the rules and routed to the people who decide, as in our accounts-payable and audit-review builds.

Private AI

Models and search in your own cloud tenant or servers, tested on your own documents before launch.

Standards

What every system includes.

  1. 01

    Auditable evidence

    Answers quote their sources. Reviews, referrals and changes are recorded.

  2. 02

    Data residency

    The system runs in the environment you choose, with access limited to the people agreed in the plan.

  3. 03

    Accessibility

    Keyboard, screen-reader and contrast testing from the first screen, with WCAG 2.2 AA as the target.

  4. 04

    Pre-launch testing

    Tested on questions and records from your own work, including the ones a person should answer.

  5. 05

    Ongoing support

    Monitoring, updates and re-testing when documents or models change.

  6. 06

    Flexible roles

    We can lead a project or provide the technology within a wider project team.
