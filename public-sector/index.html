How a first project runs

Start small, measure it, then decide what comes next.

  1. 01DefineOne document set or workflow, one team, and the measures that decide whether it worked.
  2. 02TestRun it on questions and records from your own work, including the ones a person must answer.
  3. 03ReviewCompare the results with the agreed measures, then decide whether to expand.

Standards

What every system includes.

  1. 01

    Auditable evidence

    Answers quote their sources. Reviews, referrals and changes are recorded.

  2. 02

    Data residency

    The system runs in the environment you choose, with access limited to the people agreed in the plan.

  3. 03

    Accessibility

    Keyboard, screen-reader and contrast testing from the first screen, against WCAG 2.2 AA. That covers the WCAG 2.1 AA level in the ADA Title II rule and the Section 508 standards.

  4. 04

    Pre-launch testing

    Tested on questions and records from your own work, including the ones a person should answer.

  5. 05

    Ongoing support

    Monitoring, updates and re-testing when documents or models change.

  6. 06

    Flexible roles

    We can lead a project or provide the technology within a wider project team.
