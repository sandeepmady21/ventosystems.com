What we build for agencies

Three starting points.

Document assistants

Answers from your own manuals

Staff ask questions in plain language and get answers quoted from the agency’s own manuals, standards and specifications, with the source page shown beside the answer. Questions the documents cannot answer are referred to a person.

Workflow software

Documents checked and routed

Software that receives documents, checks them against the rules and sends exceptions to the people who decide: the approach behind our accounts-payable and audit-review builds.

Private AI

Models in your own environment

Models and search that run in the agency’s own cloud tenant or servers, tested on the agency’s own documents before they go into use.

How we work with agencies

What every system includes.

  1. 01

    Evidence you can audit

    Answers quote their sources. Reviews, referrals and changes are recorded, so the agency can check how the system behaved.

  2. 02

    Data where your rules say

    The system runs in the environment the agency chooses, with access limited to the people agreed in the plan.

  3. 03

    Accessible from the start

    Screens are planned and tested with keyboard, screen-reader and contrast checks, with WCAG 2.2 AA as the target.

  4. 04

    Measured on your documents

    Before launch, the system is tested on questions and records from your own work, including the ones a person should answer.

  5. 05

    Support after launch

    Monitoring, updates and re-testing when documents or models change, by a support team that knows the system.

  6. 06

    On its own or within a team

    We can lead a project or provide the technology within a wider project team.
