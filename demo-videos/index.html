Demo Videos

Product demo videos recorded from your real app, not mock-ups, with narration, captions and every AI answer checked before it makes the cut.

Plan a video How it works

How it works

Each step is checked before the next one starts.

  1. 01ScriptThe story and every line, written with you. Nothing is recorded until the script is approved.
  2. 02RecordYour real app is driven in a browser and recorded at double resolution, with a calm, visible cursor.
  3. 03CheckEach shot is checked for names that must not appear and labels that must. Live AI answers are checked, and re-recorded if they fail.
  4. 04NarrateA natural voice, timed to each shot. Lines are rewritten to fit, never sped up.
  5. 05CutLandscape and vertical cuts, captions, chapters and a clean version, delivered to your folder.

What you get

Full demo

A narrated walkthrough of a few minutes, with chapters, burned-in captions and a clean version, up to 1440p.

Short cut

A 30 to 60 second version in landscape and vertical, with captions sized for social feeds.

Stills for sign-off

A contact sheet of every shot before the final cut, so you approve the picture before the voice.

Standards

What every video keeps to.

Real app
Recorded from the running product, with your sample data. No mock-ups or edited screens.
AI answers
Live answers are checked against what they must and must not say before a shot is kept.
Names
Client names and anything else you list are checked on every final frame.
Waits
Long waits for AI answers are shortened, and the closing card says so.
Takes
Every take is kept. Re-recording a shot never overwrites an earlier one.
Uses
Sales demos, bid submissions with chapters that reference requirements, launches and social posts.
