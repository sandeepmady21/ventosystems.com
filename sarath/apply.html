Apply

Applications for articleship, and for positions for qualified Chartered Accountants, are made by email. Articled training and any position the firm has listed are described on the Careers page.

How to apply

Choose the button for what you are applying for. It opens your email app with a message to the firm that lists what to include. Fill it in, attach your CV, and send it.

Apply for articleship

Apply for a position

If a button does not open an email app, write to info@sarathcas.in from your usual email account, and include:

  • your name and telephone number
  • the office you would prefer, if any
  • your ICAI registration number: students give their student registration number, members their membership number
  • for articleship, the stage you have reached
  • your CV, attached, preferably as a PDF

Please do not include confidential information about the clients or matters of any firm you work with. The firm uses your application only to consider it. How it handles email is set out in the privacy notice.
