The firm

Rooted in experience.
Engaged in the detail.

Established by CA P. Sarath Kumar in Hyderabad, Sarath & Associates is a firm of Chartered Accountants registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.

Audit and assurance is at the core of our practice. Today, 13 partners work across six offices, with engagements spanning listed and unlisted companies, non-corporate groups, and government organisations and agencies.
