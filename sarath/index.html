The firm

Sarath & Associates is a firm of Chartered Accountants registered with the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (Firm Registration No. 005120S). The firm was established in Hyderabad in 1990 by CA P. Sarath Kumar.

Its core practice is audit and assurance. The practice also covers direct and indirect taxation, forensic accounting and investigation, and financial advisory. Clients include listed and unlisted companies, non-corporate groups, and government organisations and agencies across a range of industries.

Engagement teams are staffed by qualified professionals who keep current with accounting and auditing standards and applicable regulations. The firm has 13 partners and works from six offices: Hyderabad, which is the head office, and Mumbai, Delhi, Vijayawada, Chennai and Bengaluru.

Practice areas

All services

  • Audit and assurance

    Internal audit and process-risk reviews, management and risk assurance, concurrent, stock and information-systems audits of bank branches, and systems and procedures reviews.

  • Taxation

    Direct and indirect tax planning and advisory, goods and services tax, compliance reviews, and representation before tax authorities.

  • Forensic accounting and investigation

    Forensic audits, fraud and misappropriation investigations, transaction audits under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016, anti-fraud process reviews, and training.

  • Advisory

    Due diligence, valuation, project financing and feasibility studies, restructuring, mergers and acquisitions, and insolvency professional services.
