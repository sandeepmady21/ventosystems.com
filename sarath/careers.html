01 / Students

Articled training

The firm trains articled assistants under the Chartered Accountants Regulations, 1988. Work is drawn from the firm's audit and assurance, taxation, forensic and investigation, and advisory practices, and assistants move between areas as the work of their office allows. Eligibility, the stipend and the period of training are as prescribed by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India.
