Privacy notice

How this website works, and how the firm handles the information you choose to share.

Using this website

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. Its fonts, images and scripts are served with the website; it makes no requests to any third party server.

Scripts support the site's navigation and prepare the application email on the Careers page. They do not track visitors or send form information to a server.

Writing to the firm

The button on the Contact page and the application form on the Careers page both open your own email app with a message to the firm already written out. Nothing is sent until you press Send in your email app, and what you send goes from your email account to the firm’s mailbox. Your message is not collected or stored by this website.

The firm uses what you send only to reply to you and, in the case of an application, to consider that application. It is not shared with anyone outside the firm except where the firm is required by law to do so.

Please do not include confidential information in an email unless the firm has asked for it.

Questions about your data

To ask what personal data the firm holds about you, or to ask for it to be corrected or erased, write to info@sarathcas.in.
