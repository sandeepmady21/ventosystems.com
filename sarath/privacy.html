Privacy notice

This website does not set cookies and does not use analytics, advertising or tracking scripts. It makes no requests to any third party server, and it runs no JavaScript.

The site has no forms and collects nothing. The buttons on the Contact and Apply pages simply open your own email app with a message to the firm. Whatever you then send, including any curriculum vitae you attach, goes directly from your email account to the firm’s mailbox. It does not pass through this website or any other company.

The firm uses what you send only to reply to you and, in the case of an application, to consider that application. It is not shared with anyone outside the firm except where the firm is required by law to do so.

Please do not include confidential information in an email unless the firm has asked for it.

To ask what personal data the firm holds about you, or to ask for it to be corrected or erased, write to info@sarathcas.in.
