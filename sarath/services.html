Audit and assurance

  • Statutory audits under the Companies Act, 2013 and other statutes, including audits of banks, insurers and co-operative societies.
  • Statutory and internal audits of listed and unlisted companies, government entities and banks, including bank branch audits, and of co-operative societies, partnership firms, limited liability partnerships, Hindu undivided families, Section 8 companies, charitable trusts and other associations.
  • Audits of government agencies and externally aided projects.
  • Bank audit services: statutory central audit, statutory branch audit including specialised, industrial finance and corporate finance branches, concurrent audit, treasury and forex branch audit, stock audit, information systems audit, and specialised monitoring assignments.
  • Internal audit and process risk services, management and risk assurance, corporate risk mitigation, and systems and procedures reviews.
  • Information systems audit.
  • Certification and attest reports.

Forensic accounting and investigation

The firm undertakes forensic accounting and investigation assignments for central and state investigation agencies, financial sector regulators, economic offence wings of state police, anti-corruption bureaus, banks and corporates.

The forensic practice covers fraud and misappropriation investigations for police and corporates; forensic audits of listed companies and of public offers for regulators; forensic audits of collective investment and chit fund schemes; forensic audits of non-performing accounts to identify diversion of funds, and joint investigative audits for corporate debt restructuring, for public sector banks; and transaction audits under the Insolvency and Bankruptcy Code, 2016 on behalf of resolution professionals in the power, manufacturing, infrastructure and real estate sectors.

  • Preventive fraud risk management studies for corporates.
  • Anti-corruption advisory and investigations.
  • Vigilance and anti-fraud investigations.
  • Tax related support in investigation matters, including fraud, tax evasion and other white collar crime investigations.

Partners deliver training on forensic accounting, white collar crime and anti-corruption investigation for law enforcement, regulatory and banking training institutions.
