Accessibility

We want this site to work for everyone, whatever device or assistive technology you use.

Our aim

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA.

What we check

Every page is checked for text contrast against the AA ratios, for layout at phone, tablet and desktop widths, and for working links, headings and page landmarks.

Menus open and close from the keyboard, and Escape closes them. Focus is always visible.

Product clips are short and silent. They only play while on screen, never play for people who have asked their device to reduce motion, and can be paused.

Known limits

We have not yet had an independent audit, and we have not tested with every screen reader. Some product screenshots contain small text; the copy around each one describes what it shows.

Tell us about a problem

If anything on the site is hard to use, write to hello@ventosystems.com with the page and what happened. We will reply and fix what we can.
