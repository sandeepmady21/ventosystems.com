AP Automation

How it works

  1. 01CaptureInvoices arrive by email, upload, API or supplier portal. Every field is read with its confidence and source.
  2. 02MatchTwo-way and three-way matching at line level against purchase orders and receipts, within tolerances you set.
  3. 03ResolveExceptions get a root cause, an owner, a deadline and a recommended next step with its evidence.
  4. 04Approve and recordPolicy-based approval, then posting, with every step written to a tamper-evident audit trail.

Features

01

Matching you can read

Each invoice is matched line by line to its purchase order and receipts, and the result is explained in plain language, so a reviewer sees why it passed or failed.

  • Two-way and three-way matching
  • Tolerances by supplier, entity, category and amount
  • Confidence on every extracted field
02

Exceptions with a next step

Every exception has a root cause, an owner and a deadline, plus a recommended action that shows the evidence behind it. Requests to receivers or suppliers are drafted and tracked.

  • 28 exception types, each with an owner and deadline
  • Recommended action with confidence and evidence
  • Drafted requests, tracked in one place
03

Approvals by policy

Invoices route by entity and amount through sequential steps, with extra review when risk is high. Reminders and escalation run on a schedule.

  • Automatic approval for low-risk matched invoices, within limits you set
  • Delegation, approval limits and segregation of duties
  • Scheduled reminders and escalation
04

An audit trail you can verify

Each event records who or what acted (a person, a rule, the system or an AI step) and the confidence behind it. The log is append-only and hash-chained, so any change is detectable.

  • Append-only, hash-chained event log
  • AI steps labelled with confidence and explanation
  • Verification of the full chain

Common questions

Does it replace our ERP?

No. It works ahead of your ERP: invoices are captured, matched and approved here, then posted to the ERP, which stays your system of record.

Which ERPs does it connect to?

It has one adapter for SAP S/4HANA and ECC, Oracle, Dynamics 365, NetSuite and Workday, tested against simulated systems. The live connection is set up and tested with each client.

Where is AI used, and can we check it?

Matching, coding and risk steps show their confidence and the evidence behind them, and you set the thresholds for automatic, review and manual handling. Every step is recorded in the audit trail.

Can it handle several entities and currencies?

Yes. Access is scoped by legal entity, and invoices can be in USD, GBP, INR and other currencies, with VAT and GST.
