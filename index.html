How we deliver

One team, from first scope to support.

  1. 01

    Scope on real work

    Start from one workflow, one set of documents or one site, and agree what success looks like before building.

  2. 02

    Build with evidence

    Suggestions link to their source records. Testing uses your own examples, including the cases a person should decide.

  3. 03

    Support what we ship

    Monitoring, updates and repeat testing are part of the plan, with a support team that knows the system.
