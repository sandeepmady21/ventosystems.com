Services

What we do for clients

We build AI systems for a company’s own operations, using the same methods behind our products.

Train

Models trained on your data

Where a general model falls short, we train one on the examples only your company has. Before it goes into use, we measure it against a test set agreed with you.

Host

Hosted where you choose

Models run in your cloud account or on your own servers. When a model from a large provider is the better fit, we recommend it and explain the trade-off.

Connect

Connected to your systems

The system reads from SAP, your ERP, databases and document stores. Changes it proposes go to a person for approval before anything is written back.

Sense

AI on physical sites

Systems that read camera, sensor and location data to track assets and people on a site, detect incidents and alert the staff who need to respond.

Check

Results that show their working

Outputs come with the sources, rules and calculations behind them. Actions are written to an append-only log that an auditor can read.

Why Vento

Much of a company’s work still runs on documents, spreadsheets and people checking each other’s numbers. AI can take on a large share of it when it is built on the company’s own data and its results can be checked. These are the rules we build by.

01

We measure before we build

We start with a sample of your real work and agree how success will be measured. If AI is the wrong tool for the job, we tell you then.

02

Your data stays with you

Systems run in your environment. You decide what is stored and for how long.

03

A person signs off

Decisions that matter go to someone on your team for approval, and each action is logged.

04

You own what we build

Code, models and test sets sit in your accounts from the start. You can run, retrain and check the system without us.
