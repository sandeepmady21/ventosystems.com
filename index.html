Why Vento

We build AI that acts on a company’s own data, in its offices and on its sites. The same rules apply whether a system approves a supplier invoice, sends staff to someone who needs help, or moves a truck on a haul road. These are the rules we build by.

01

We measure before we build

We start with a sample of your real work or a trial on your site, and agree how success will be measured. If our technology is the wrong tool for the job, we tell you then.

02

Your data stays with you

Software, models and sensor data stay in your environment. You decide what is stored and for how long.

03

A person stays in charge

People approve the decisions that matter and can stop a machine at any time. Each action is logged.

04

You own what we build

Code, models and test results sit in your accounts from the start. You can run, retrain and check the system without us.
