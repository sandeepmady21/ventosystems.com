Why Vento

Much of a company’s work still runs on documents, spreadsheets and people checking each other’s numbers. Software and AI can take on a large share of it when they are built on the company’s own data and their results can be checked. These are the rules we build by.

01

We measure before we build

We start with a sample of your real work and agree how success will be measured. If our technology is the wrong tool for the job, we tell you then.

02

Your data stays with you

Systems run in your environment. You decide what is stored and for how long.

03

A person signs off

Decisions that matter go to someone on your team for approval, and each action is logged.

04

You own what we build

Code, models and test sets sit in your accounts from the start. You can run, retrain and check the system without us.
