Five delivery models

What the team owns

Give the team a piece of work that matters to your business. That could be taking responsibility for an enterprise integration, shipping a product workflow or running part of a platform.

Before hiring, agree who sets the roadmap, what the team can decide and how it will work with the people who depend on it. A clear mandate gives you a better way to evaluate candidates and review the first release.

For a longer-term India operation, include local leadership, how the team will be employed and which functions could follow the first.

Our model

Agree on the work, build the team around it, and use delivery reviews to decide what to take on next.

1. Hire

Start with real tasks the team will face. For an integration role, ask a candidate to explain how they would validate source data, handle failures and test the result. Agree with your leads how each role will be assessed.

How we plan hiring

Agree on sourcing, technical assessment, client interviews and who approves each hire. Set out who will employ the team and provide day-to-day support.

A useful first scope

Name the deliverable, who will review it and the technical decisions the team should own.

2. Integrate

Set up approved access to your issue tracker, repositories and environments. Use a first piece of work to test the handoffs from planning and code review through to release and acceptance.

How we agree operating boundaries

Document data access, work-product ownership, release approvals and escalation. Assign payroll, equipment and workspace responsibilities to the client, Vento or an agreed local provider.

3. Review and grow

Set a regular review for what shipped, defects, blockers and upcoming priorities. Choose measures that fit the work, such as integration failure rates or release reliability. Use the results to decide where the team needs more capacity or leadership.

When the plan includes a handover

Agree who would receive the team or center, what can transfer and who signs off. Cover people, agreements, access, records and support after handover.

Hiring process

Use these stages to agree on who assesses each role, when your team joins in and what the engineer needs to start.

  1. 01

    Define the role

    Describe the first assignment, core skills and the decisions the engineer will make.

  2. 02

    Source candidates

    Agree where to look and what evidence to use for the initial shortlist.

  3. 03

    Screen for fit

    Check relevant experience, working preferences and the needs of the team.

  4. 04

    Assess the work

    Use a relevant work sample or technical discussion to examine trade-offs, testing and communication.

  5. 05

    Interview with your team

    Have your leads discuss the work with shortlisted candidates and weigh in on the decision.

  6. 06

    Approve the hire

    Confirm the hiring decision, employment arrangement and local support.

  7. 07

    Onboard to the first task

    Set up access, introduce reviewers and start with work the team can take through acceptance.
