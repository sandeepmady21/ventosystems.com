What the city offers

Software & product engineering

The city’s technology ecosystem spans product development, enterprise software, cloud, data and AI. That gives buyers a starting point for evaluating the specialist roles their roadmap calls for.

Workspace choices

HITEC City, Madhapur, Gachibowli and the Financial District are among the established office districts. Consider commute, planned team size and security requirements when assessing a managed workspace or dedicated office.

Research & prototyping

IIIT Hyderabad’s research centers span data science, machine learning, robotics and embedded systems. The local ecosystem also includes T-Hub for startup support and T-Works for hardware prototyping.

Local setup

Before you hire

The first team

Name the initial mandate, lead role and specialist skills. Define how candidates will be evaluated and who makes the hiring decision.

Workspace & access

Decide which work calls for an office and which can be hybrid or remote. Agree who provides equipment, grants access and supports the team.

Working with headquarters

Choose shared hours for planning, decisions and handoffs. Set written conventions for reviews, releases and escalations across time zones.

Local operating responsibilities

Decide who employs the team, runs payroll and handles workspace and local administration. A client-owned center can also call for specialist entity and compliance support.
