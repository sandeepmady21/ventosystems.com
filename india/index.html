Common questions

What is the difference between an ODC and a GCC?

An ODC typically owns software engineering or development work. A GCC can have a broader mandate across engineering, data and other functions, with dedicated leadership and governance.

Who employs the team and owns its work?

For Team Extension and Dedicated Teams, Vento Systems Pvt Ltd employs the engineers and you direct their work. For larger centers the employer depends on the model you choose, and Build–Operate–Transfer moves the team to your own entity. The work belongs to you: ownership, repository access and confidentiality are set out in the engagement terms before anyone starts.

Can we start with one team and expand later?

Yes. Start with a defined assignment and agree how to review it. Use that review to decide whether to add roles, a second squad or another function.

What affects timing and cost?

Role mix, seniority, hiring availability, access requirements and the local operating scope all shape the plan. A first conversation can identify the largest dependencies.

How do working hours line up with our time zone?

Shared hours for planning, reviews and handoffs are agreed when the team is set up, along with written conventions for everything else.

Can we take the team over later?

Yes. Build–Operate–Transfer sets out how the team or center is set up, run and handed over to your own entity when you are ready.
