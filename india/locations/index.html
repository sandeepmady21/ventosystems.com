Before you hire

Decisions to settle for any location.

The first team

Name the initial mandate, lead role and specialist skills, how candidates are evaluated and who makes the hiring decision.

Workspace and access

Decide which work needs an office and which can be hybrid or remote, and who provides equipment, access and support.

Working with headquarters

Choose shared hours for planning, decisions and handoffs, with written conventions for reviews, releases and escalations.

Local operating responsibilities

Decide who employs the team, runs payroll and handles workspace and administration. A client-owned center may also need entity and compliance support.
