Working together

Understand the domain. Engineer the system.

Start with one workflow and representative examples. We can scope the problem, prototype a difficult step or develop the system, with inputs, integrations and review criteria agreed for the first stage.

01

Map the workflow

Identify the documents, systems, decision owners and recurring exceptions. Establish the volume of work and time spent so the first scope addresses a specific problem.

02

Test the difficult step

Use representative examples to test the approach before expanding it. Review the data connections, permissions and exceptions with the people doing the work.

03

Evaluate the result

Compare the result with the starting point. Use the findings to agree what needs refinement, what can be integrated and whether a wider rollout is justified.

Choosing a first scope

Which workflow is worth improving first?

Look for a process with recurring documents, clear decision owners and exceptions that delay the next step. Describe how it works today, where information is re-entered and what would make the outcome easier to review.
