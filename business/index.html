Travio

Importers need to turn material records into trade classifications and duty estimates, with enough context for a reviewer to make the decision.

Travio connects trade workflows, SAP material data and a review interface. Our client project brings classification suggestions, supporting references and the reviewer’s decision into a traceable workflow.

AP Automation

An invoice has to connect to a purchase order, an approval and the records a finance team relies on. Exceptions can stall that process.

AP Automation brings document processing, matching and approval workflows together. The build receives supplier invoices, checks purchase-order information and routes cases for a decision.

03

AuditProof

Audit findings need to connect back to the records behind them. The professional reviewing a finding needs to inspect the evidence and resolve questions.

AuditProof combines financial-data review with an interface for investigating findings. It links the result to underlying records and keeps the professional’s judgment central to the workflow.

04

Practice Tracker

Accounting firms need to see what is missing, who is waiting and what can move forward. Clients need a clear request and a simple way to respond.

Practice Tracker brings practice operations, interaction design and workflow modelling together. The Document Chaser demo connects the firm’s chase board and document checklist to a client view, using fictional data and simulated actions.

05

Operations systems

A pharmaceutical distributor has to coordinate purchasing, stock, sales and payments while keeping track of batches and expiry dates.

Our client system combines inventory modelling, operational software and team permissions. Procurement, batch and expiry records, sales, invoicing and payments share a common workflow, with roles for the people responsible at each step.

Working together

Understand the domain. Engineer the system.

Domain knowledge, system design and evaluation meet in the same development process.

01

Understand the handoffs

Map the documents, systems and decisions involved. Establish the volume of work, time spent and recurring exceptions before choosing what to automate.

02

Build around real examples

Use a representative sample of the work to test the approach. Connect to the systems the team already uses, with clear permissions for each action.

03

Review the outcome

Put the workflow in front of its users, inspect the exceptions and compare the result with the starting point. Expand when the evidence supports it.
