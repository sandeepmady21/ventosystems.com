Client projects and prototypes

Systems built for specific clients, and prototypes we are developing.

Tarivo

Client project · Trade compliance

Importers need trade classifications and duty estimates from their material records, with a basis a reviewer can inspect. Tarivo connects SAP material data, classification suggestions and duty estimates in one review interface, with supporting references beside each suggestion.

Discuss Tarivo

Distribution Operations

Client project · Pharmaceutical wholesale

A pharmaceutical distributor has to coordinate purchasing, stock, sales and payments while tracking batches and expiry dates. Vento built software linking procurement, inventory, sales, invoicing and payments, with batch and expiry tracking and permissions for stock, sales and finance staff.

Discuss a similar system

Practice Tracker

Prototype · Accounting firms

Accounting firms need to see what is missing, who is waiting and what can move forward, and clients need a simple way to respond. The Practice Tracker prototype tracks blocked work and document requests, with a client upload page that needs no login.

Try the prototype

AuditProof

Prototype · Audit workflows

Professionals reviewing audit findings need to trace them to the underlying records. AuditProof combines financial-data review with an interface for investigating findings, linking each finding to its supporting records for the professional to judge.
