Starting a conversation

A route to specialist expertise.

A useful first conversation connects your concern to a defined environment, a clear objective and the team responsible for delivery.

01

Describe the environment

Outline the applications, infrastructure and data involved, and the people who use them. Describe the question you want to resolve, such as access to a system, a planned integration or a change in how data is handled.

02

Agree the scope and team

Discuss the question to investigate, the work involved and the team or specialist partners responsible. Agree the scope, expected outputs and commercial terms for the engagement.

03

Make the next step clear

Identify who can explain the system, who will review the outcome and any timing or access constraints. Agree the information needed for the next conversation before sharing detailed material.
