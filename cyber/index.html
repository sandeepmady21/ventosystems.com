The relationship

A route to specialist expertise.

A useful first conversation connects your concern to a defined environment, a clear objective and the team responsible for delivery.

01

Describe the environment

Outline the applications, infrastructure and data involved, and the people who use them. Explain what prompted the enquiry: an existing concern, a planned change or a requirement you need to understand.

02

Agree the scope and team

Discuss what the work should establish and which company or specialist partners would deliver it. The scope and commercial terms should identify the responsibilities for the engagement.

03

Make the next step clear

Identify who can explain the system, who will review the outcome and any timing or access constraints. Agree the information needed for the next conversation before sharing detailed material.
