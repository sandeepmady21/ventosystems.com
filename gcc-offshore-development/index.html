Which model fits your plans?

Offshore development center (ODC)

Build a dedicated offshore engineering team ↗

For product and technology leaders who need ongoing engineering capacity. Define the software, integrations or AI-assisted workflows the team will take on and how it will work with your engineers.

Global Capability Center (GCC)

Plan a capability center in India ↗

For companies establishing a long-term India operation. Define the center’s mandate, first functions, local leadership and operating model. Decide what ownership should look like as it grows.

How the engagement works

Plan the work

Agree on the first area of work, the skills it calls for and a first milestone. Identify who owns the roadmap and makes delivery decisions.

Set up the team

Define roles, technical interviews, onboarding and system access. Agree who handles hiring, employment, payroll and local operations before setup begins.

Run and review

Set a cadence for planning, code review and delivery reviews. Use the results to decide when to expand the team or take on another function.

Questions to discuss before setup

Can we start with one team?

Yes. Start with one area of the roadmap and decide whether to add roles or functions once the team is delivering. If you already know you need a broader India operation, GCC planning can begin with that mandate.

Can we take over the operation later?

If you want to own the center, discuss transfer during planning. A Build–Operate–Transfer arrangement depends on the scope, who employs the team, and the agreed conditions and timing for handover.

What would your India team take on first?

Share the work the team would own, the skills you need and when you want to start. We can use that to scope the first stage together.
