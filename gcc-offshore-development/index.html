Before you start

Questions worth settling early.

What is the difference between an ODC and a GCC?

An Offshore Development Center typically owns engineering and development work. A Global Capability Center can have a wider mandate across engineering, data or business functions, with its own leadership and governance. An ODC can be the starting point for a broader center.

Who directs the team and owns its work?

Agree who controls the roadmap, hiring approvals, release decisions and the repositories. Define ownership of source code, documentation, models and data in the engagement terms, along with access and handover requirements.

Who handles local employment and operations?

The operating model determines the employer and who handles payroll, workspace, devices and local administration. A launch plan should identify which responsibilities sit with your organization, Vento or a specialist partner.

How do we handle security and production access?

Start with your data and security requirements. Agree the identity and device controls, environments, approval paths and access removal process before the team starts working in your systems.

What affects timing and cost?

Scope and cost depend on role mix, seniority, hiring availability, tooling, workspace and the local operating support needed. Start with the first team and milestone so estimates reflect the work and dependencies.

Can we bring the operation in-house later?

Discuss a transfer at the planning stage if eventual ownership matters. Define what would move, including people, tooling, documentation and operating responsibilities, and agree on the conditions and approvals for the handover.

What should your India team take on first?

Share the first area of work, the skills you need and your target start. We can use that to discuss the right model and the decisions needed before launch.
