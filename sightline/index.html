Sightline

Prospecting, email, calls and deals in one workspace for B2B sales teams, with every task saying why it is on today's list.

See it work

Real screens from the working build, with invented demo data.

How it works

  1. 01FindBring in people and companies from lists or a Salesforce export, each record keeping its history.
  2. 02Reach outSequences mix automatic emails, emails to review, calls and LinkedIn reminders, sent inside each person's working hours.
  3. 03TalkCall from the browser with call prep and a calling-hours check. Replies land in an inbox sorted by what they mean.
  4. 04CloseDeals move on a board with a reason for every win or loss, and reports show plan, coverage and renewals.

Features

01

Email you review before it sends

Any step in a sequence can wait for a person. A suggested opening line shows what it is based on and stays out of the email until you accept it, and an email with a missing field will not send.

  • Sends only inside the recipient's working hours
  • Daily caps for every mailbox
  • Replies, bounces and unsubscribes stop a sequence
  • No open or click tracking
02

Calls from the browser

Work through a calling list with call prep drawn from your own records. Every call is checked against the person's local time and place first, and unanswered calls log themselves.

  • Calling-hours rules for 12 countries and 8 US states, each citing its law
  • A short countdown, then the next call
  • Call outcomes logged in one click
03

Deals that need a next step

Open deals by stage, with next steps, quiet deals and missing next steps flagged. Won and lost both need a reason, and a won deal makes the company a customer.

  • Next steps and stars on every card
  • On-hold deals with a return date
  • Reps move their own deals; managers can move anyone's
04

Reports a manager can act on

Won against plan, coverage of what is left, renewals at risk and what changed since last week. People set the forecast categories; the system does not predict them.

  • Eight report screens and 70+ defined metrics
  • Plan for and could reach
  • Currencies never added together

Common questions

Does it replace our CRM?

It can. Sightline keeps companies, people, deals and activity, and imports a Salesforce data export as a one-time move. It does not sync with Salesforce.

Does it track email opens?

No. There is no open or click tracking. Replies, bounces and unsubscribes are what count.

Where is AI used?

For suggested opening lines, call prep and sorting replies. Each suggestion shows what it is based on, and nothing goes into an email until a person accepts it. With AI off, rule-based versions take over.

Can we try it?

Yes. A demo with six months of sample history shows a rep's day and a manager's view.
