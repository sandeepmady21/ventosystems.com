Vento Private AI

AI that fits your environment.

Private AI brings model evaluation, company knowledge and deployment engineering together. We scope this work for each application, starting with a defined task, the data it needs and how the result will be tested.

The engineering

Connect the model to the system.

An engagement starts by evaluating the task and deployment requirements. The findings guide whether to develop a system, and which models, integrations and infrastructure it needs.

01

Measure the model on your work

Build a representative set of tasks from the intended workflow. Compare candidate models on the result the team actually needs, including cases that should be referred to a person.

Assessment & evaluation

02

Plan where it runs

Connect model requirements to the infrastructure: expected usage, response times, available compute and operating costs. Evaluate servers or cloud deployment alongside access and retention requirements.

Deployment design

03

Connect company knowledge

Bring the right records and context into the workflow through retrieval, data integration or model adaptation. Preserve the connection to source information and evaluate changes against the same task set.

Model & workflow development

04

Make it maintainable

Treat the model, its sources and the surrounding application as one system to maintain. Define monitoring, updates and repeat evaluation, with documented dependencies and responsibilities for handover.

Operational handover

Company knowledge, within reach.

We are exploring private search across company documents, tickets and meeting notes, with attention to source references and the permissions attached to the underlying material.

Transcription close to the source.

On-device transcription is another area of exploration: capturing and summarising speech locally. Hardware and model choices would need evaluation against practical recording conditions.

How it connects

Infrastructure in service of an outcome.

The same engineering questions appear in finance workflows, internal knowledge tools and site applications: what does the model need to know, where does it run and how is its output used?

01

A defined boundary

Agree which information can enter the system, who can access it and which external services, if any, are permitted.

02

A measurable standard

Keep representative evaluation examples and review results when models, prompts or source data change.

03

An explicit handover

Document licensing, access, ownership and ongoing support for the particular system being delivered.
