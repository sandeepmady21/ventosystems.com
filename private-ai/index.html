How it works

From a first assessment to a system your team can maintain.

  1. 01EvaluateCompare candidate models on a representative set of your tasks, including the cases a person should take.
  2. 02Plan deploymentCompare on-premises and cloud options on usage, response time, compute and cost.
  3. 03Connect knowledgeBring your records in through retrieval or integration, keeping the link to each source.
  4. 04MaintainMonitoring, updates and repeat evaluation, with documented handover.

What an assessment delivers

Model comparison

Candidate models compared on your own tasks, measured on the result your team actually needs.

Deployment recommendation

Where it should run, with access, retention and permitted external services defined.

Implementation plan

How the chosen approach connects to the workflow, with evaluation and handover agreed in scope.

What we need from you

Three inputs to scope a useful assessment.

Task
One recurring question or decision, the expected result, who reviews it, and where a person takes over.
Information
The records involved, how they are accessed and any limits on where they may be processed.
Operating conditions
Expected usage, response-time needs, available infrastructure and who will maintain the system.
Terms
Licensing, ownership and support requirements to agree in scope.
