The engineering

Engagement scope

An assessment can bring together a comparison of model options, results on your tasks and a deployment recommendation. An implementation connects the chosen approach to the workflow, with evaluation and handover agreed in scope.

01

Evaluation

Build a representative set of tasks from the intended workflow. Compare candidate models on the result the team actually needs, including cases that should be referred to a person.

Assessment & evaluation

02

Deployment planning

Compare on-premises and cloud options against expected usage, response times, available compute and operating costs. Define access, retention and permitted external services before choosing the deployment.

Deployment planning & implementation

03

Knowledge integration

Bring the right records and context into the workflow through retrieval, data integration or model adaptation. Preserve the connection to source information and evaluate changes against the same task set.

Model & workflow development

04

Maintenance

Treat the model, its sources and the surrounding application as one system to maintain. Define monitoring, updates and repeat evaluation, with documented dependencies and responsibilities for handover.

Preparing an assessment

Scoping an assessment

Start with a description of the work and its constraints. These three inputs help us scope a useful comparison before choosing a model or deployment.

01

Task

Describe one recurring question or decision, the expected result and who will review it. Include examples of where a person needs to take over.

02

Information

Describe the records involved, how they are accessed and any limits on where they may be processed. Account for prompts and system records as well as source documents.

03

Operating conditions

Outline expected usage, response-time needs, available infrastructure and who would maintain the system. Identify licensing, ownership or support requirements to agree in scope.
