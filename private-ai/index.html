Services

Private AI

Your own AI model, trained for your field and running inside your walls, with no internet connection if your work requires it.

Request an assessment How we build it

Yours, in three ways

Built for your field, run inside your walls, and owned by you.

01

Built for your field

A model trained on how your industry works, so it gives the specific answer your team needs. Smaller and cheaper to run than a general-purpose model.

02

Inside your walls

On your own servers, in your data center, or in an air-gapped network with no internet connection. Nothing goes to an outside AI service unless you allow it.

03

Owned by you

You keep the model and everything it learns. Your experts’ corrections become training data, and the model carries forward to newer base models without starting over.

Where it fits

Organizations whose most valuable data cannot leave the building.

  • Defense and aerospace suppliers
  • Banks and insurers
  • Hospitals and health systems
  • Energy and utilities
  • Manufacturers
  • Public agencies

Common first uses: answers from your own documents, drafts and summaries, and data read from forms and records.

How we build it

From your experts’ knowledge to a model you run yourself.

  1. 01Capture the workTurn real examples of your workflow, and the answers your experts expect, into tests the model must pass.
  2. 02Train for your fieldAdapt a model to your domain with your data and your experts’ corrections, measured against those tests.
  3. 03Deploy inside your wallsInstall it on your servers, in your data center or in an air-gapped network, sized to the hardware you have.
  4. 04Keep improvingMistakes in production become new training data, and improvements carry forward to newer base models.
