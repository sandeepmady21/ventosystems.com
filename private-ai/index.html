Services

Private AI

AI that runs on your own servers or in your own cloud account, so sensitive documents and data stay under your control.

Request an assessment How we set it up

What teams use it for

Everyday work with documents, where the data cannot leave your control.

Answers from your documents

Ask a question and get an answer quoted from your policies, contracts or manuals, with the source alongside.

Drafts and summaries

First drafts of reports, letters and case notes, and summaries of long documents, for a person to review.

Data from documents

Fields read from invoices, forms and records into your systems, with anything uncertain flagged for a person.

Why run it privately

Many organizations cannot send contracts, patient records or case files to a public AI service. A private setup keeps them where they already live.

  • Runs on your servers or in your cloud account
  • Access follows the permissions you already have
  • Outside services only if you allow them
  • Tested on your own documents before launch

How we set it up

From a first assessment to a system your team can run.

  1. 01AssessTry candidate models on real examples of your task, including the cases a person should handle.
  2. 02Choose where it runsCompare your own servers and cloud options on speed, cost and who can reach the data.
  3. 03Connect your documentsBring in the records it needs, keeping the link back to each source.
  4. 04Run and supportMonitoring, updates and re-testing when your documents or the model change.
