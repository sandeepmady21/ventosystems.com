Vento Safety

Help people respond when it matters.

We are developing ways to connect sensing, operational software and staff response on physical sites. A first pilot would be scoped with a partner around one area and one use case.

In development · Pilot enquiries

Pilot scope

Understand one area before expanding.

A pilot would test the signal, the information it carries and the response that follows. Scope and success measures should reflect the conditions of the site.

01

Location and check-in

Explore how a pass, wristband or phone can help staff understand where someone was last seen. Define participation, coverage and access to location data for the particular site.

02

Signals that deserve attention

Test camera and sensor inputs against a clearly defined event. Review missed events and false alarms under realistic conditions before relying on alerts in an operational workflow.

03

Alerts and response

Put relevant context in front of the people handling the incident. Track acknowledgement and response so the pilot can show whether the workflow helps staff act sooner.

04

Equipment monitoring

Explore sensor-based monitoring for equipment and facilities, starting with a defined fault pattern and a clear action for the team receiving the alert.

Exploration

The approach

Design with the people on site.

The technology has to work within the site’s existing response procedures and operating responsibilities.

01

Set the pilot boundary

Choose one area and one use case. Agree what will be observed, who will participate and what the pilot is expected to demonstrate.

02

Measure what happens

Record the starting conditions, then evaluate coverage, alert quality and staff response. Include the cases where a signal is missing or uncertain.

03

Review before expanding

Use the findings to decide what should change and whether the next stage is justified. Keep the operator involved in that decision.

Data & participation

Make the boundaries clear.

Define how people participate, who can view site data and how long it is retained. Deployment and access should reflect the setting and the needs of its operators.
